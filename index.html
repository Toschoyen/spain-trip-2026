  • €100 fine if you enter the pool without washing sunscreen off first — it kills the algae system. Rinse before you swim.

  • No trash bins at the villa — carry trash ~100m to the city containers on the street (by the roundabout). Mixed and recycling marked separately.

Section 02 · Where we're staying

The villa
& the spot.

Rojales sits between Torrevieja and Guardamar — south of Alicante, north of the Mar Menor. Quiet inland village, beach 10 minutes away, salt lakes 15.

📍 Villa · Calle, 3, Rojales 03170
Rojales is an inland Spanish village, not a tourist strip — meaning quieter nights, real local shops, and no Brits-on-tour energy at the front door. The Mediterranean is a 10-minute drive (Guardamar beach), Torrevieja's harbor and bars are 15 minutes south, and most of the activities in this doc are within a 30-minute radius. The villa has a pool, sleeps 8, AC throughout, and a kitchen — see Section 01 for the rules.
